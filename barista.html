Becoming a Barista

At Starbucks, we believe in nurturing talent and providing opportunities for growth. Becoming a barista is not just about making coffee; it's about creating memorable experiences for our customers.

As a barista, you'll learn the art of crafting high-quality beverages, mastering the use of espresso machines, and understanding the nuances of different coffee blends. Some of the core recipes you'll master are explained in the list below.

Espresso Bar Drinks

These are hot (or sometimes iced) drinks built around espresso + steamed milk / foam / water, etc. They tend to emphasize coffee flavor, milk balance, and texture.

Cappuccinos

Build / composition: espresso + steamed milk + a generous layer of foam

Taste profile: strong espresso-forward, slightly mellowed by milk; foam lightens the mouthfeel

Texture / mouthfeel: airy, light foam on top, creamy body underneath

Distinctive quality: noticeable foam layer; can be “dry” (more foam) or “wet” (less foam)

Americanos

Build / composition: espresso + hot water (diluted)

Taste profile: coffee-like in strength but retaining espresso character (more body & flavor than drip)

Texture / mouthfeel: smooth, without milk or foam (unless customized)

Distinctive quality: simple, clean, espresso-forward without milk masking

Flat Whites

Build / composition: espresso (ristretto — means “restricted” in Italian. In the context of coffee, refers to a short shot of espresso, meaning the barista pulls only the first portion of a full-length espresso shot.) + steamed milk with microfoam (thin foam)

Taste profile: strong espresso balance with milk, less diluted than a latte

Texture / mouthfeel: velvety, silky microfoam — “painted” milk over espresso

Distinctive quality: very thin foam, strong espresso presence, smooth integration of milk and coffee

Shaken Espressos

Build / composition: espresso + syrup + milk (or milk alternative) + ice, all shaken

Taste profile: bright, crisp espresso with sweetness and slight froth from shaking

Texture / mouthfeel: light, effervescent feel from shaking, chilled

Distinctive quality: cold and lively; shaking brings small bubbles and a brisk character

Lattes

Build / composition: espresso + steamed milk + thin layer of foam

Taste profile: creamy, smooth, milk-forward with balanced espresso flavor

Texture / mouthfeel: rich, velvety, with a light foam topping

Distinctive quality: larger milk proportion; can be flavored with syrups or sauces

Cold Bar Drinks

These are iced, blended, or cold-infused drinks. They lean more toward refreshment, sweetness, texture play, and sometimes fruit or cream contrasts.

Frappuccinos

Build / composition: ice + milk (or milk alternative) + coffee or crème base + flavored syrups / sauces + toppings (whip, drizzle)

Taste profile: like a coffee milkshake (in coffee versions) or dessert-style drink (in crème versions)

Texture / mouthfeel: thick, creamy, smooth, blended

Distinctive quality: frozen consistency, dessert appeal, often topped with whipped cream

Refreshers

Build / composition: fruit juices or concentrates + water or lemonade + green coffee extract (light caffeine) + ice + fruit inclusions (e.g. freeze-dried fruit)

Taste profile: fruity, bright, lightly sweet, refreshing

Texture / mouthfeel: light, crisp, cold and hydrating

Distinctive quality: no milk; strong fruit-forward character; visually appealing with fruit bits

Cold Brew

Build / composition: cold-brewed coffee (slow steeped in cold water) + ice + optional additions (milk, sweet cream, foam, syrup)

Taste profile: smooth, mellow, less acidic than hot brew, clearer coffee flavor

Texture / mouthfeel: clean, light to medium body, sometimes creamy if milk/foam added

Distinctive quality: deep coffee flavor, chill-forward, versatile for cream or foam layering
